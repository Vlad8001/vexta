FAQ

Common questions

What is a booked meeting?

It is when a decision maker who fits your profile has confirmed they want to talk, and the meeting is already in the calendar. Not a vague "sounds interesting, message me sometime".

Do you really work under our brand?

Yes. All messages go from your company and your own domain. To the client it looks like your in-house team.

When do the first results come?

First replies start within a week of launch. First meetings usually appear in weeks 3-6.

What if there are no meetings?

On the pilot we agree on 2-3 meetings. On monthly work we guarantee at least 9 per quarter, or we keep working for free until we reach that number.

How much does it cost?

The pilot is $349. Then packages from $500 to $1200 per month, plus $75 per booked meeting. Minimum 3 months.
